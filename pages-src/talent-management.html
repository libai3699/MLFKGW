2025 Corporate Sustainability Report

Talent Management

Since Artisan Partners was founded in 1994, our success as a global multi-asset investment platform has been predicated on having talented associates throughout the organization in every role, at every level. We understand that attracting, developing and retaining talented professionals is an essential component of our business strategy.

Our Culture Artisan Unity Compensation and Benefits

Our Culture

We are committed to providing an ideal home that is attractive to our current and prospective associates and that allows our talented associates to thrive throughout the course of their careers at Artisan.

Our culture promotes associates’ success, ideally over long careers. We believe this investment in our people is instrumental in delivering best-in-class investment outcomes for our clients. Our people-focused mindset is instilled through our onboarding process, reinforced by leadership, and reiterated through ongoing corporate communications and everyday corporate operations.

Artisan Unity

Our success as an active investment manager is predicated on differentiated thought and creative perspectives. As a talent-driven organization, we invest significant energy in the recruitment of our associates as they are critical to ensuring the long-term success of our firm. We recognize talent exists everywhere, and we are dedicated to identifying and advancing the best talent that will flourish within our business model.

Artisan Unity advances our commitment to a culture that supports engagement, collaboration and the celebration of our associates. Our ongoing efforts foster a merit-based culture that encourages mentorship and strengthens connection and communication across the firm.

Of our 567 associates in total, 40% are women while 21% are ethnically diverse1. We believe our culture of investing in our associates is reflected in our average associate tenure of eight years. We strive to recruit and hire outstanding associates who thrive in broad roles and want the freedom to grow their talents and careers. We are committed to seeking professionals from different backgrounds, experiences and geographies to foster creative thinking and differentiated perspectives. We have long-standing relationships with a variety of recruitment partners and community organizations to broaden our candidate pools and increase our access to talent. We focus on providing a professional environment where our associates can be successful over a long-term time horizon, ideally throughout their careers.

Our commitment to human capital development takes many forms, including supporting ongoing education and professional development, offering a flat management structure with easy access to senior managers, and working closely with associates on career goals and interests to create development pathways and opportunities for advancement.

Our Associate Affinity Groups (AAGs) provide spaces for connection, support, education and dialogue. As collaborative networks, they encourage connectivity, enhance professional and personal growth, and promote unity across our community. Our first affinity group was founded more than 10 years ago, and we now have seven AAGs that continue to enrich our workplace and support our associates.

2025 Associate Affinity Groups (AAG) Highlights

Artisan’s AAGs continued to foster connection, strengthen our culture, and create opportunities for associates to learn, contribute, and engage both inside and outside the firm.

CORE (Communication Outreach Recognition Events)

Mission

Strengthen community across the firm by fostering cross-functional connections, social engagement and collaborative initiatives.

2025 Highlights

Kept momentum strong with a full slate of connection and community-building moments. As leaders of Artisan’s intersectional AAG events, they played a key role in offering numerous volunteer events for associates. Their signature events such as a summer outing to cheer on the Milwaukee Brewers, team trivia, annual AAG Fair and bag toss tournament brought associates together in meaningful, memorable ways.

diffAbilities

Mission

Promote awareness, understanding and empowerment around visible and invisible differences to reduce stigma and foster inclusion.

2025 Highlights

Continued advancing awareness and inclusion through programs that spotlight lived experience and community partnership, including continued participation in On Your Marq program, a college success program for neurodivergent students, in addition to leading Artisan’s participation in The UnstopABLE Cup, a wheelchair basketball tournament in support of The Ability Center.

The Green Team

Mission

Inspire environmental awareness and sustainable action through education, volunteerism and collective engagement.

2025 Highlights

Turned sustainability into action by leading the elimination of single-use utensils from our café. They drove broad associate engagement through high-impact initiatives, including a clothing swap co-hosted with Women’s Netowrking Initiative (WNI), a composting workshop, an e-cycle and carbon footprint reduction campaign, along with volunteer partnerships with Milwaukee Riverkeepers.

Multicultural Exchange (MCEx)

Mission

Celebrate and elevate diverse cultural perspectives through open dialogue, shared experiences and meaningful engagement.

2025 Highlights

Helped strengthen firmwide connection and community, participating alongside other AAGs in shared events—most notably the AAG Fair—making it easy for associates to discover groups, build relationships and stay connected across teams.

Pride Alliance

Mission

Create a safe, collaborative space where LGBTQIA+ associates and allies can thrive authentically while advancing education and inclusion across the firm.

2025 Highlights

Delivered a vibrant Pride Month experience celebrating its fifth anniversary as an AAG. Associates connected through a happy hour, a “Meet the Alliance” social and trivia event, and a closing Popsicles on the Patio gathering while members engaged in discussions, learning opportunities, and feedback sessions to foster open dialogue and shared input.

Women’s Networking Initiative

Mission

Connect women and allies to build supportive relationships that advance personal growth and career development.

2025 Highlights

Fostered dialogue and shared learning through a Women’s History Month panel and continued building community with intersectional programming and a barre class celebrating National Women’s Health and Fitness Day. Their efforts culminated in the release of the WNI Favorite Things list—a firm-favorite, crowdsourced year-end gift guide featuring recommendations from women across Artisan.

The Young Professionals Network (YPN)

Mission

Empower emerging professionals to connect, collaborate, and grow through shared knowledge and network-building.

2025 Highlights

Supported early career growth with high-impact development opportunities, including multiple offerings in their industry bootcamp series, supporting the community through the SCORES Cup and social activities. We helped Artisan’s rising professionals learn and grow together with the support of the whole firm.

Compensation and Benefits

As an ideal home for talent, we believe it is critical that we provide compelling work in a positive environment, with long-term opportunities for associates across our firm as well as economic alignment and comprehensive benefits.

Our compensation program provides associates with transparency as to how performance measurement translates into compensation. Our goal is to create compensation and incentive programs that attract global talent and inspire them to do their best work here. Associates are evaluated based on their contribution to the firm and rewarded in the form of variable compensation and long duration incentive awards. As a meritocracy, we believe associates who create more value for the firm should be provided with greater rewards.

We believe our thoughtful approach to compensation practices aligns associate values with the long-term strategic goals and interests of the firm for the overall benefit of our clients, resulting in associates thinking and acting as long-term owners of our business. We regularly review compensation paid to associates to ensure it is competitive, equitable and fair for the role, experience, location and individual contribution.

We offer holistic benefits that support physical, mental and financial wellness for our associates and their families. Associates benefit from comprehensive health care, generous paid time off, and robust retirement savings programs.

We continue to emphasize health and wellness for all employees. We cover 100% of our participating associates’ and their dependents’ qualifying healthcare costs, which, in the US, amounted to approximately $22,800 per associate in 2025, based on total annual premium costs and year-end enrollment. Approximately 1,600 associates and dependents were covered under Artisan’s health insurance plan during 2025. In addition, the firm contributes to associates’ Health Savings Accounts in excess of annual deductible amounts, helping offset eligible medical, dental and vision expenses.

We encourage our associates to save for retirement and promote financial wellness. Artisan’s 401(k) Plan offers a 100% match on pre-tax and/or Roth 401(k) contributions up to the IRS elective deferral limit, with immediate vesting. In 2025, 98% of US-based associates participated in the 401(k) plan, with 67% contributing the IRS maximum amount of $23,500. Additionally, Artisan covers 100% of plan administration fees, provides an in-plan Roth rollover option for voluntary, after-tax contributions, and free financial concierge services.

Intellectual curiosity and passion for learning are fundamental competencies at Artisan. We actively support associate learning and development, both formally and informally, and encourage advancement from within the firm. We provide associates with the tools they need to succeed and advance their careers. Our tuition reimbursement program is available to associates who are pursuing applicable undergraduate and graduate degrees, certifications and licenses relevant to the business.

Equitable benefit offerings are provided for non-US-based associates.
