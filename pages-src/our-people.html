Our People

Our People Are Our Greatest Asset

As a talent-driven business, providing both an energizing, inspiring and stable home for our people is paramount to our success. Artisan Partners’ culture is rooted in diversity of thought and creative perspectives. We are committed to a diverse and inclusive environment that empowers our associates to be their authentic selves and reach their full potential.

Corporate Culture

Artisan’s culture has always been centered on our people. Our culture promotes associates’ success, ideally over their entire careers. We believe this investment in our people is instrumental in delivering best-in class investment outcomes for our clients. Our “People First” mindset is fundamental to Who We Are as an organization and is present throughout the entire associate journey. Our associates aren’t a consideration within our business—they are our business.

Meet Our People

We have the best and brightest people in the industry—people who are incredibly intelligent, passionate about our business, dedicated to our investments first culture and willing to stretch themselves to get results for the firm—and most importantly for each other.

Our people are talented, dedicated, and engaged.
