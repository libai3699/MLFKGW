Investor FAQs

Getting Started

How do I invest in an Artisan Partners Fund?

Select the appropriate account application to open an Artisan Partners Fund account. If you do not see the form or application you need, contact us at 800 344 1770.

Artisan Partners Funds are also available with various third-party brokerages firms as part of a No Transaction Fee (NTF) program. Inquire directly with the brokerage firm of your choice regarding Artisan Partners Fund availability and account fees.

What are the investment minimums to open an account in an Artisan Partners Fund?

Artisan Partners offers multiple share classes for its mutual fund lineup. Share class availability varies per fund.

Investor Shares

No load share class offered to members of the general public. Investment minimum: $1,000.

The initial investment minimum for Investor Share Classes is waived if you invest thorough an Automatic Investment Plan.

Advisor Shares

No load share class generally available for investment only by employee benefit plans, clients of financial advisors, clients of sponsored fee-based programs and other investors that meet the minimum investment requirements, which typically contain less expenses than the Investor Share Class, if applicable. Investment minimum: $250,000.

The minimum investment requirements will be waived on (1) certain employee benefit plans that hold their shares through plan-level or omnibus accounts on behalf of participants; or (2) investors who purchase shares through financial intermediaries where (i) the intermediary has entered into an agreement with Artisan Partners Funds or Artisan Partners Distributors LLC and (ii) the intermediary holds shares through an omnibus account, although the intermediary maintaining such an account may impose its own minimum investment requirements.

Institutional Shares

No load share class designed for certain employee benefit plans as well as institutional and other investors who are able to meet the high minimum investment requirements, which typically contain less expense than the Investor or Advisor Share Class, if applicable. Investment minimum: $1,000,000.

The minimum investment requirements will be waived on (1) certain employee benefit plans that hold their shares through plan-level or omnibus accounts on behalf of participants; or (2) investors who purchase shares through financial intermediaries where (i) the intermediary has entered into an agreement with Artisan Partners Funds or Artisan Partners Distributors LLC and (ii) the intermediary holds shares through an omnibus account, although the intermediary maintaining such an account may impose its own minimum investment requirements.

For additional information on each Fund’s available share class expenses, minimum balances and eligibility requirements, refer to the Fund's prospectus or contact us at 800 344 1770.

What type of account can I open?

How do I obtain a medallion signature guarantee?

A medallion signature guarantee may be obtained from an officer at your commercial bank, broker-dealer, credit union (if authorized by state law), securities exchange or association, clearing agency, or savings association. An acceptable guarantee uses a STAMP2000 Medallion imprint appropriate for the nature and size of the transaction and has the words "SIGNATURE GUARANTEED" stamped or typed near your signature on this form.

Note: A Notary Public stamp or seal is different from a signature guarantee and is not acceptable in certain situations.

Online Account Access

How do I register for online account access?

Visit our Account Access page to register your account for online access.

Please note: If your account is not held directly with Artisan Partners Funds (i.e. If your account is through a third party), you will need to contact that firm for assistance. If you are unsure, we recommend checking your account statements.

How do I change my Username for online account access?

Sign in to your account via our Account Access page. Click on Account Profile, then click Change Username. Enter your current password, then input your NEW Username and confirm it. Click Submit. A confirmation screen will appear.

How do I change my Password for online account access?

Sign in to your account via our Account Access page. On the Account Profile, then Click Change Password. Enter your CURRENT Password, then Enter your New Password and confirm it. Click Submit. A confirmation screen will appear.

What happens if I get locked out of my account?

Contact a customer service representative at 800 344 1770 to have your account password reset. Please have your account number and Username available for identification purposes.

What can I do via online account access?

Through online account access, you can:

  • View/Download Daily Confirmations, Statements, and Tax Forms
  • Enroll in E-Delivery for Electronic Notifications (Fund Documents, Daily Confirmations, Statements, and Tax Forms)
  • Update Your Address
  • Update Your Email Address
  • Change Your Username/Password
  • Change Your Distribution Options
  • Change Your Cost Basis Method/Retrieve Average Cost Basis Information
  • Order Daily Confirmations and Statements
  • View Your Account History
  • Update Security Profile

E-Delivery

What is E-Delivery?

Reduce clutter in your mailbox and help the environment by signing up for E-Delivery, a free service for shareholders who prefer receiving fund documents in an electronic format.

What documents are available through E-Delivery?

Fund documents available through E-Delivery include daily confirmations, account statements, tax forms, prospectuses, summary prospectuses, annual and semi-annual reports, privacy statements and special announcements.

To view account information online enroll in Account Access.

How do I consent or enroll for E-Delivery?

Go to Account Access. In the Account Setting menu select e-Delivery Preferences. Here you can choose which items you’d prefer to receive via email. You’ll need to agree to the Terms and Conditions and click submit. You should receive both a confirmation screen and a confirmation email.

How do I update my email address for E-Delivery?

To update your email address for E-Delivery, visit Account Access. In the Account Setting menu select e-Delivery Preferences, input your new email and confirm it. Then choose which items you’d prefer to receive via email. You’ll need to agree to the Terms and Conditions and click submit. You should receive both a confirmation screen and a confirmation email.

You should receive both a confirmation email to both your new and old email address.

How do I manage my preference for e-Delivery?

Visit Account Access. In the Account Setting menu select e-Delivery Preferences. Here you can choose which items you’d prefer to receive via email or unsubscribe if you prefer. You’ll need to agree to the Terms and Conditions and click submit. You should receive both a confirmation screen and a confirmation email for the choices you’ve made.

My spouse and I have individually registered accounts with Artisan Partners. If I enroll in E-Delivery, will I eliminate paper documents for both of our accounts?

E-Delivery enrollment is based on each shareholder's SSN/TIN, therefore only the accounts tied to that specific SSN/TIN will be enrolled in E-Delivery. A separate enrollment must be made for additional SSN/TINs in the same household.

What happens if I do not receive any email notifications?

If E-Delivery notifications are rejected, Artisan Partners will make periodic attempts over the course of the next three days to resend the email. After three days, E-Delivery enrollment will be discontinued and you will be notified by regular mail to reactivate your E-Delivery preferences. Otherwise, regular mail delivery of paper documents will commence with the next mailing.
